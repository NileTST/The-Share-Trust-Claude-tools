# Voice

Drafts represent The Share Trust to a real donor — warm, personal, specific, not mail-merge-flavored. Use the donor's name and, where it reads naturally, something from their giving history (e.g. the fund their last gift supported) — don't force in every data point just because it's available. The project-update sentences should read like news for someone who already cares, not a pitch. Keep it short: a check-in, not a newsletter. No guilt or pressure about having lapsed. If the signing staff member/fundraiser isn't obvious from the data, ask rather than invent one.
