# AI Policy — Relevant Rules

Source: TST Acceptable Use of AI Policy v1.0.

- Looking up donor data and drafting outreach in the org's CRM (Blackbaud) is an approved use — this is donor communications, not the "non-sensitive data only" research use that other TST routines are restricted to.
- Donor PII may be processed here but never committed to git, pasted into unrelated tools, or left anywhere outside `donor-lists/` and `drafts/`.
- This routine never sends. Every draft is reviewed and sent by a human — that review is the required AI-use disclosure/verification step for content reaching an external recipient.
- If a request asks this routine to auto-send, message donors outside the supplied CSV, or use donor data for anything besides drafting this outreach, decline and flag it.
