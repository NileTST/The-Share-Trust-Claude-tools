# Acronyms

## TST & WAC
- LCA: Local Coalition Accelerator — first tier of TST/WAC core strategy
- PtB: Passing the Buck — TST research report on local funding dynamics
- RPPL: Research, Programmes, Policy, Learning — TST internal standards and tools framework
- SHG: Self-Help Group — first tier of TST/WAC core strategy
- TST: The Share Trust
- WAC: Warande Advisory Centre — TST's deep-partnership affiliate

## Programs & Initiatives
- AFNS: Aid Fund for Northern Syria — multi-donor pooled funding mechanism (FCDO)
- BRCi: Building Resilient Communities in Somalia (consortium)
- CLA: Cluster Level Association — groups of SHGs in an LCA (Ethiopia)
- FLA: Federal Level Association
- STAAR: Social Protection Technical Assistance, Advice, and Resources Facility (FCDO)
- VSLA: Village Savings and Loan Association
