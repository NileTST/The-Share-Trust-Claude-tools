# TST Localization Evidence Scan — October 2026

**Period covered:** 1–31 October 2026
**Run date:** 1 November 2026
**Audience:** TST strategy team and general staff. Internal use only.

**Sources reviewed (7):** Center for Global Development (CGD); Peace Direct; TAI (Transparency and Accountability Initiative); MFAN; ALNAP; NEAR Network; U.S. Department of State.

**Search guidance applied:** locally led development; locally led action; localization; intermediaries; alternative pooled funds; complementary.

## Summary

October 2026 produced little new, verifiable content on the search topics. Two of the seven sources (Peace Direct, ALNAP) published October items that engage with locally led or decolonial practice. These are borderline against the guidance because they concern knowledge systems and research practice rather than funding, intermediaries or pooled funds. No October item was verified for CGD, TAI, MFAN, NEAR or the U.S. Department of State. No new content on intermediaries, alternative pooled funds or "complementary" approaches was found in the window.

## Limitations of this scan

Collection used web search and page retrieval. Several source pages could not be read directly (CGD returned HTTP 403 or 404; State Department returned HTTP 403; NEAR and TAI pages were empty or returned an error). Absence of a finding for those sources means none was verified, not that none was published. Staff should check those sites directly.

## Cross-cutting theme: decolonising and locally led practice (borderline relevance)

*Editorial note: This is grouped as a cross-cutting theme because two sources published on it in the window. Neither item addresses funding flows, so relevance to TST's core guidance is limited.*

### Peace Direct
- **"Locally led Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL): A Decolonial Approach"** (21 October 2026). Peace Direct presents a guide developed with local partners since 2021. It argues that existing MEL systems do not capture the real impact of peacebuilding and that MEL should place Global South voices and knowledge at the forefront. It offers dialogue tools and a step-by-step guide for local peacebuilders and international organisations to co-develop context-specific MEL frameworks, in English, French, Spanish and Arabic. The page does not address localization funding, intermediaries or pooled funds. https://www.peacedirect.org/locally-led-mel-guide

### ALNAP
- **"Decolonising humanitarian health: A scoping review of practical guidance"** (2 October 2026; contributed by 60 Decibels, hosted in the ALNAP library). A scoping review of practical guidance on decolonising humanitarian health research and practice, addressing colonial power dynamics and equity. Only the page abstract was reviewed. https://alnap.org/help-library/resources/decolonising-humanitarian-health-a-scoping-review-of-practical-guidance/
- **CartONG commentary on the effect of AI on do-no-harm, localisation and humanity principles** (19 October 2026; French title "Quel impact de l'IA sur les principes du ne pas nuire, de la localisation et d'humanité ?"; hosted in the ALNAP library). The page gave no summary, and the content was not reviewed. Listed because the title engages with localisation. https://alnap.org/help-library/resources/quel-impact-de-lia-sur-les-principes-du-ne-pas-nuire-de-la-localisation-et-dhumanit%C3%A9/
