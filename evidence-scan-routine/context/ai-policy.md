# AI Policy — Relevant Rules

Source: TST Acceptable Use of AI Policy v1.0, effective June 1, 2026.

- Approved uses include research and synthesis on non-sensitive organizational data and publicly available information.
- This routine must never process PII, confidential organizational data, partner-sensitive data, or research participant data.
- AI-generated content for external publication requires human review, verification, and AI-use disclosure. This routine's outputs are for internal use; if any output is later shared externally, that requirement applies.
- If a request to this routine touches PII, participant data, or confidential/partner-sensitive data, decline and flag it rather than proceed.
