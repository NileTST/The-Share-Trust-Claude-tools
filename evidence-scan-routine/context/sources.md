# Approved Sources

This is the working list of sources the routine is allowed to search. Edited only via `/add-source` and `/remove-source` commands (see routine-prompt.md).

1. **Center for Global Development (CGD)** — Washington, D.C.–based think tank producing research and policy analysis on international development, aid effectiveness, and global development finance.
2. **Peace Direct** — International peacebuilding organization that supports and advocates for locally led peacebuilding, often publishing research and commentary on shifting power to local actors.
3. **TAI (Transparency and Accountability Initiative)** — A funder collaborative and learning hub focused on transparency, accountability, and civic participation in development and governance.
4. **MFAN (Modernizing Foreign Assistance Network)** — A U.S.-based coalition advocating for reform and modernization of U.S. foreign assistance policy and practice.
5. **ALNAP (Active Learning Network for Accountability and Performance in Humanitarian Action)** — A learning network of humanitarian organizations producing research and evaluation on humanitarian effectiveness, including localization.
6. **NEAR Network (Network for Empowered Aid Response)** — A network led by local and national actors that advocates for locally led humanitarian and development action and more direct, equitable funding.
